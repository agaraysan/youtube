Privacy Policy

quiz-uploader · Effective 2026-11-16

quiz-uploader ("the tool") is a private, internal tool used only by its owner, an individual YouTube creator, to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. The tool uses YouTube API Services.

1. Information the tool accesses

With the owner's permission given through Google OAuth 2.0, the tool accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel. It uses the scopes needed to upload videos and to manage the owner's own videos: video files, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, privacy status and scheduled publish times. It does not access, collect or store data about any other person.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to upload the owner's videos and to set or change their details and privacy status. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics about other people.

3. Storage and security

The OAuth refresh token and client credentials are kept in GitHub encrypted secrets in the owner's private repository. Upload results (video ID, time and status) are kept as logs in that private repository. Nothing is shared with or sold to third parties.

4. Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

5. Cookies

This website and the tool do not use cookies or tracking.

6. Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be sent through the contact options on the owner's GitHub profile, github.com/agaraysan.
